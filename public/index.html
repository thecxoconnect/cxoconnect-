Privacy Policy

1. Introduction
The CXO Network, operated by HRSG Solutions Global, respects executive privacy. This policy outlines how your business contact data is handled.

2. Information We Collect
We collect professional credentials submitted during application or account activation (name, designation, corporate email, company, contact details, areas of interest).

3. Use of Information
Your information is used strictly to curate executive engagements, facilitate roundtables, manage event access for the 30 October Conclave, and communicate membership updates. We do not sell or rent member data to third parties.

4. Security
All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard cryptographic algorithms. Data transmissions are protected with Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Terms of Membership

1. Eligibility
Membership in The CXO Network is extended to senior decision makers, C-suite executives, and business leaders across approved enterprise tiers. Applications are subject to peer review by the membership committee.

2. Code of Conduct & Confidentiality
Members agree to observe the Chatham House Rule during closed-door roundtables and conclave discussions. Insights and viewpoints shared during private sessions remain confidential.

3. Event Access • 30 October Conclave
Active members in good standing are entitled to delegate entry to the Annual Leadership Conclave on 30 October 2026. Transfers must be requested in advance via the membership team.

4. Governance & Deactivation
The membership team reserves the right to deactivate accounts that breach the code of conduct or professional standards.
